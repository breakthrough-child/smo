Show Me Once

Show a phone task once on video, and AI turns it into an onboarding-style tour your parents can follow alone, on any app.

“Show it once. They've got it forever.”

The unique kernel

One recording → a tour built from real screenshots of the real app, with a spotlight on exactly what to tap and the instruction read aloud. Their screens, the way the helper actually did it. Works for any app.

Who it's for

👩 Helper (creates)Adult child who keeps getting called to explain the same task. Today: explaining over the phone, frustrating for both.
👵 Learner (follows)Parent or grandparent, not confident with phones. Needs big text, one step at a time, a clear “tap here”, spoken instructions.

The core loop

  1. Record: helper uploads a 3–90 s screen recording (or 2–15 screenshots) + task name.
  2. AI builds: picks the real steps, finds what was tapped, writes plain instructions.
  3. Review: move the circle, edit text, delete a step, publish.
  4. Send: the link on WhatsApp.
  5. Parent follows alone: spotlight, read aloud, “Got it, next” → “You did it!”. Reopens the link whenever they forget.

What “working” looks like

  • A real WhatsApp recording → published tour with correct steps; only minor circle moves.
  • Both phones tested for real: an iPhone recording and an Android recording each → correct published tour. If either video can't be decoded, the friendly message appears and that phone's screenshots work via the fallback.
  • Tour works end to end in a phone browser, with audio.
  • Deployed on Vercel at a real public URL.
  • Friendly messages for undecodable video, AI failure, no steps found, missing guide.
The “oh, that's cool” moment: their parent opens the WhatsApp link on her own phone and follows the tour — spotlight and voice — without help.

The boundary

NOW The proof of concept
  • Create: video with client-side frame extraction, or 2–15 screenshots; task name.
  • AI: server-side Claude vision → strict JSON, zod-validated, one retry; low-confidence steps flagged.
  • Review: drag-to-move circle, edit text, delete step, publish; sensitive-content banner; “don't record passwords” notice.
  • Share: link, “Send on WhatsApp”, copy link.
  • Tour: spotlight, pulsing ring, large tooltip, speech + “Hear again”, Back/Next, progress, completion.
  • Unguessable links; edit token in the creator's browser.
  • “Delete this guide” — checks the edit token, removes the row and its frames.
  • Vercel deploy; README; scope/prd/spec committed.
LATER Stretch
  • Circle resize — drag-to-move only for now.
CUT And why
  • Accounts/login — the edit token is enough for one helper.
  • Payments, analytics — don't prove the kernel.
  • Live overlay, native apps — the web tour over real screenshots is the idea.
  • Multiple languages — one proves the loop.
  • Editing beyond move/text/delete — review fixes small misses only.
  • Guide lists, dashboards — one guide per link is the flow.

Feel

Warm, calm, reassuring, never techy — soft colors, rounded shapes, a friendly display font. “A patient person sitting next to you.” Big text, big buttons, high contrast, light and dark, reduced motion respected.
